3. Quantified Diagnostic Findings
  • Screening Consumes 34.4% of Process: Recruiters lose 16.5 days in initial resume review alone, exceeding the entire interview phase (14.0 days).
  • Cycle Lag Destroys Offer Acceptance (r = -1.00): As time-to-hire stretched from 38d to 56d, offer acceptance collapsed from 83.3% to 53.8%.
  • Job Boards Create Volume Mirage: 42.8% of applications come from job portals, but convert at only 1.9% and drive 40.8% of all early exits (31 of 76).
  • Recruiter Overload Bottleneck (r = 1.00): Mike Ross & James Cooper manage 55% of all requisitions, suffering a 55.3-day time-to-hire.

5. Actionable Handoff to Task 2 (Solution Engineering):
  1. Automated AI Resume Screening: Deploy intelligent parsing & pre-screening to cut the 16.5-day screening stage down to < 48 hours.
  2. Recruiter Capacity Rebalancing: Cap individual recruiter allocations at 25 open requisitions to restore SLA compliance.
  3. Sourcing Channel Pivot: Expand employee referral incentives and recalibrate job portal screening rubrics to eliminate 90-day retention failure.

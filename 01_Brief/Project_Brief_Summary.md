---

## 1. Executive Summary & Context
TalentBridge is facing severe hiring friction characterized by extended hiring cycles, candidate drop-off at the final stages, recruiter burnout, and disappointing early retention. The **HireSmart** initiative has been established to systematically diagnose, streamline, and modernize TalentBridge's talent acquisition ecosystem. 

**Task 1** serves as the foundational diagnostic and organizational phase. It establishes governance, deploys collaboration infrastructure, audits raw hiring data across a 12-month period, quantifies systemic bottlenecks, and delivers a rigorous 1-page Hiring Data Insights Report to guide subsequent solution engineering (Tasks 2+).

---

## 2. Core Problem Statements
TalentBridge's talent acquisition pipeline suffers from four interconnected operational breakdowns:
1. **Extended Time-to-Hire (48 Days Average):** The recruitment cycle from initial requisition/application to offer acceptance stands at an unsustainable 48 days (industry benchmark: 28–32 days), driving significant top-talent attrition.
2. **Declining Offer Acceptance Rate:** The percentage of candidates accepting offers has experienced a steep downward trajectory over recent quarters, wasting substantial recruiter and hiring manager interview investment.
3. **Severe Manual Screening Burden:** Recruiters spend excessive hours manually vetting non-standardized resumes with minimal automated pre-filtering, creating massive pipeline congestion.
4. **Elevated Early Employee Exits:** A critical proportion of newly hired personnel depart within their first 90 days of employment, reflecting alignment mismatches and onboarding friction.

## 5. Budget & Business Constraints
* **Task 1 Infrastructure Budget:** **$0 (Zero-Cost Tooling Constraint)**. Workspace and analytical infrastructure must leverage free-tier enterprise solutions (Google Drive, Notion, Google Sheets/Python, Looker Studio/Matplotlib, Gemini/OpenAI free tiers).
* **Tooling Requirements:** All prompts and AI-assisted workflows must be fully recorded in a centralized Prompt Log to guarantee ethical AI governance and verifiable data integrity.
* **Non-Goals for Task 1:** Task 1 strictly isolates diagnostics. It explicitly excludes developing custom AI models, altering company compensation bands, or restructuring formal HR policies.
